apply Op component-wise to two operands

Covers all combinations of Vector3D (any depth) and scalar operands, e.g. Vector3D<int> + Vector3D<double>, Vector3D<Vector3D<int>> * Vector3D<double> or double - Vector3D. At least one operand has to be a Vector3D.

true if T has to be indexed (T[i]) when combined element-wise with Other

An operand is indexed if it is a Vector3D that is at least as deep as the other operand. The shallower operand (a scalar or a less nested Vector3D) is broadcast to every component instead.
