◆ takeStep()

void engine::RingPolymerQMMDEngine::takeStep ( )
overridevirtual

Takes one step in a ring polymer QM MD simulation.

The step is taken in the following order:

  • First step of the integrator
  • Apply thermostat half step
  • Run QM calculations
  • couple ring polymer beads
  • Apply thermostat on forces
  • Second step of the integrator
  • Apply thermostat
  • Calculate kinetic energy and momentum
  • Apply manostat
  • Reset temperature and momentum

Reimplemented from engine::MDEngine.
