◆ getMMMolecules() [1/2]

template<typename Derived>
auto molsys::SimulationBoxView< Derived >::getMMMolecules ( )

get all MM molecules using range-based filtering

Returns
a view/iterator of MM molecules filtered from all molecules

This function returns a range-based view that filters molecules from _molecules based on whether they are designated as molecular mechanics (MM) molecules. The classification depends on the job type:

  • MM-only simulations: all molecules are MM molecules
  • QM-only simulations: no molecules are MM molecules
  • Hybrid QM/MM simulations: active molecules are MM molecules
