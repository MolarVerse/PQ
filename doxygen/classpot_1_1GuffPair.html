std::pair< double, double > pot::GuffPair::calculate ( double distance) const
nodiscardoverridevirtual

calculates the energy and force of a GuffPair

Each contribution is gated on its leading coefficient being non-zero. This skips expensive pow/exp calls for terms that contribute nothing, which is common in sparse .guff parametrizations, while keeping behavior identical for any distance > 0.
