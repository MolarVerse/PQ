Detailed Description

runs tasks one after another on a single background thread

at most capacity tasks are queued or running; submit blocks until there is room. The first exception a task throws is rethrown on the caller's thread by the next submit or wait. The destructor finishes all queued tasks and prints an error that was not rethrown to stderr.
