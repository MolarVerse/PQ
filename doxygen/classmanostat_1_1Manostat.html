QuantityTensor3D manostat::Manostat::_toSimSpaceScaling ( const QuantityTensor3D & scalingTensor,
const molsys::Box & box )
staticnodiscardprotected

transform a scaling tensor into simulation space

for boxes with fixed angles (non-orthorhombic) the scaling tensor is diagonal in orthogonal space and is transformed via h S h^-1 (equal to T S T^-1 for diagonal S). For orthorhombic boxes and boxes allowing a change of angles the tensor already acts in simulation space.

Parameters
scalingTensor
box
Returns
QuantityTensor3D representing the scaling tensor transformed into simulation space
