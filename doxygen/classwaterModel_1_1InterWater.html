Initialize the non-Coulomb pairs for the configured inter-water model.

Sets up energy and force cutoff values for the three inter-water non-Coulomb pairs (OO, OH, HH) by evaluating them at their radial cutoff distances.

◆ _setNonCoulombCutOffRadii()

void waterModel::InterWater::_setNonCoulombCutOffRadii ( ) const
private

Apply radial cutoffs to configured inter-water non-Coulomb pairs.

Uses the explicit non-Coulomb cutoff when configured, otherwise falls back to the Coulomb cutoff. O-O is always updated, while O-H and H-H are only updated when oxygen-only non-Coulomb interactions are disabled.
